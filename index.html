ABOUT NVT CODENAME: A LIFE IN BLOOM

NVT Codename: A Life in Bloom Project Overview

NVT Codename: A Life in Bloom is a garden-themed luxury villa community by NVT Quality Lifestyle, situated off Whitefield-Sarjapur Road in East Bangalore. The development is planned across approximately 10 acres with 107 villas, with Phase 1 comprising 62 exclusive villas across approximately 7 acres.

The project offers spacious 4 & 5 BHK villas with built-up areas ranging from approximately 3,750 to 4,432 sq. ft. Each residence is planned with a private backyard, an exclusive skydeck and an expansive skylight, combining generous indoor spaces with private outdoor living.

Positioned near the Whitefield, Sarjapur and Varthur employment corridors, the community also benefits from proximity to established international schools. The development is planned with over 40 lifestyle and recreational amenities, including themed gardens, landscaped spaces and dedicated areas for wellness, leisure and community activities.
